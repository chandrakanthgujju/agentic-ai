# Acme Outfitters — Return and Refund Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026. Fictional company used for the agentic-ai demo._

## Return window

- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- **Gear with electronics** (GPS watches, headlamps) has a **15-day** return window.
- Items bought during the Black Friday and holiday sales can be returned until **15 January**.

## Condition

Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached. Footwear that has been worn outdoors
cannot be returned unless defective.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and open **Orders**, choose the order, and click **Return item**.
2. Pick a reason. For damaged items, upload a photo.
3. Drop the parcel at any partner courier location within 7 days, or schedule a pickup ($5 fee,
   waived for Gold members).

## Refunds

- Refunds go to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after the warehouse
  receives the item.
- UPI and wallet refunds usually arrive within 48 hours.
- Shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective or we shipped the wrong item.

## Exchanges

Size and colour exchanges are free once per order. The replacement ships when the original is
picked up.
