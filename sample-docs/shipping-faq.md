# Acme Outfitters — Shipping FAQ

_Fictional company used for the agentic-ai demo._

**Which carriers do you use?** BlueDart for express, Delhivery for standard, and India Post for
remote postcodes.

**How long does delivery take?**

| Service | Metro cities | Other cities | Remote areas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (free over $75) | 3-5 days | 5-7 days | 7-10 days |
| Express ($12) | 1-2 days | 2-3 days | Not available |

**Can I change my address after ordering?** Yes, until the order status is `packed`. After that,
contact support and we will try to redirect the parcel with the carrier.

**My order shows `delayed`. What now?** Delays usually clear within 48 hours. If an order is
delayed by more than 5 days beyond the promised date you are eligible for a $10 store credit.

**Which countries do you ship to?** India, the United States, Ireland, Spain and Japan. Delivery
times above are for India; other countries add 2-4 days.
